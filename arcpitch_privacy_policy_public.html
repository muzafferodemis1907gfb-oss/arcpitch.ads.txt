ArcPitch Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ArcPitch, developed by Victoriumsoft, handles user information.

Information We Collect

ArcPitch is a football physics game. The app does not require users to create an account and does not collect directly identifiable personal information such as name, email address, phone number, address, or payment information.

Automatically Processed Data

The app may process limited technical data required for app functionality, performance, crash prevention, device compatibility, and local game progress. This may include device type, app version, gameplay progress, settings, scores, coins, unlocked items, and local preferences.

Local Storage

Game progress, settings, rewards, coins, and similar gameplay data may be stored locally on the user's device.

Internet and Network Access

The app may use internet and network state permissions for online-related functionality, platform services, or future service integrations. The app does not require users to log in to access its core gameplay.

Third-Party Services

If third-party services such as Google Play services, analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services are enabled in future versions, those services may process data according to their own privacy policies. Any such use will be disclosed in the app's Google Play Data safety section and this policy will be updated when necessary.

Data Sharing

ArcPitch does not sell personal data. Data is not shared with third parties except where necessary for app functionality, legal compliance, platform services, or services clearly disclosed to users.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored gameplay data remains on the user's device unless the user clears app data or uninstalls the app. Users may request privacy-related information or deletion assistance by contacting the developer.

Children

ArcPitch is intended to be suitable for a general game audience. If the app is made available to children, the developer will comply with applicable family and children privacy requirements on Google Play.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect app data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
